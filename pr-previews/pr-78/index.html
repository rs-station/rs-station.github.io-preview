What we do

Open science for the next generation of structural biology

RSS is a consortium building an open-source ecosystem for frontier structural biology, spanning X-ray crystallography, cryo-EM/ET, SAXS, and the AI that increasingly connects structure to function. We're an open community that welcomes contributions from academics, industry, and non-profits across the world of structural biology.
